# Presenting AlphaQ

Use **Log in → Try as customer**. No password or model credits are required. The sample workspace is separate from the real wholesaler and belongs to your browser's demo cookie for 24 hours. Keep the same browser tab when switching roles. Orders persist in PostgreSQL across refreshes and server instances.

The demo uses scripted order interpretation and drafts, clearly labelled in its banner. The real workspace uses the configured free OpenRouter model. Optional dashboard summaries call the free model on demand and may encounter provider limits. Dispatch, messaging and billing are simulated.

## A short walkthrough

1. **Landing, 15 seconds:** Show “for the builders”, pricing and the Live dashboard link.
2. **Customer, 35 seconds:** Try the customer login. Create an Office essentials order, review the catalogue-backed Order Mirror and confirm it.
3. **Staff, 15 seconds:** Switch roles in the banner. Show the order queue and stock. Approval and plan changes are unavailable to staff.
4. **Manager, 35 seconds:** Open the order, inspect stock before/after and the simulated customer message, then approve. Refresh to show one dispatch receipt.
5. **Customer, 25 seconds:** Switch back. Show the completed order and timeline. Submit 12 desktop staplers, confirm, and show the shortage choices.
6. **Owner, 25 seconds:** Show team permissions and plan usage. Simulate a subscription checkout; no payment details are collected.
7. **Guided tour, 15 seconds:** Show Take a tour and the step-by-step instructions for people exploring independently.

Record at 1920×1080 or 1440×900 with a clean browser window. Keep credentials, environment files and cloud consoles out of the recording. Finish on the dashboard. OAuth is optional and should not be shown as working until its real callback has been verified.
